## What This Is

A practitioner's toolkit for finance leaders managing:

- **Post-merger integration** of multiple subsidiaries into a group structure
- **PE/board-level financial reporting** with governance frameworks
- **Multi-site consolidation** across branches, franchisees, and legal entities
- **Finance BI dashboards** that provide 360-degree operational and strategic visibility

These are not academic frameworks. They come from real-world experience integrating companies post-acquisition, building financial governance from zero, and consolidating 70+ locations into unified reporting.

---

## Key Principles

1. **Process before tools** — Fix the process first, then select the system
2. **Governance by design** — Every entity follows the same close calendar, approval matrix, and reporting cadence
3. **Materiality-driven** — Don't harmonise everything on Day 1; focus on what moves the consolidated P&L
4. **Franchise-aware** — Different ownership structures (owned, franchised, JV) require different consolidation methods
5. **Audit-ready from Day 1** — Every decision documented, every mapping traceable
